Interpretation and limitations

Results apply only within this model and these tested conditions. Runs—not vehicles—are experimental replicates. Bootstrap intervals are exploratory and unadjusted for multiple comparisons; small samples can be inconclusive. Non-completed trips are censored; the full-demand loss applies an explicit one-horizon penalty. Person outcomes depend on assumed occupancy. Emissions are SUMO HBEFA model estimates.

Reproduce from the included configuration, manifests, network hashes, application revision and pinned dependencies. See METHODOLOGY.md for equations, controller deviations and sampling definitions.
